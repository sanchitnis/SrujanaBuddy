# SrujanaBuddy - Coaching Session Workflow

## Purpose

This document defines the operational coaching workflow for REVA student sessions.
It aligns to the v1.4 requirements and the orchestration logic in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
For REVA-specific value interpretation and student guidance norms, use [references/REVA University.md](references/REVA%20University.md), especially `The Strategic Directive: Educate to Enterprise`, `Spiritual and Moral Anchoring: The Gita Perspective and Universal Values`, and the student-principles section near the end of that document.

## Universal session contract

Each session ends with:
1. One immediate next step.
2. One explicit time-bound commitment.
3. One review checkpoint.

Each daily session includes dopamine baseline check:
1. Energy and mood (1-10).
2. Stimulation risk.
3. Natural reward anchor.

## Year-group routing defaults

These defaults should remain aligned with REVA's learner-centric and holistic-development framing in [references/REVA University.md](references/REVA%20University.md#L55).

1. Year 1 (Foundation): orientation, study skills, campus integration, GTD basics.
2. Year 2-3 (Development): skill depth, projects, internships, leadership growth.
3. Final Year (Transition): placements, interviews, portfolio finalization.
4. PG (Advanced): thesis cadence, research output, advanced enterprise track.

## Required session catalog (24)

### 1. Beginner interactive orientation
- Outcome: first useful plan within 10 minutes.
- Output: one 24-hour commitment.

### 2. Daily focus planning
- Outcome: clear day plan with one priority.
- Includes mandatory dopamine baseline.

### 3. Weekly Svadhyaya review and reset
- Outcome: all five spheres reviewed; weekly commitments reset.

### 4. Learning-to-learn coaching
- Outcome: retrieval-based weekly learning plan.

### 5. Assessment preparation coaching
- Outcome: exam/assignment strategy by difficulty and deadline.

### 6. Subject mastery session (Socratic)
- Outcome: misconception diagnosis and next problem set.

### 7. Socratic concept clarification
- Outcome: student-generated summary and one practice problem.

### 8. Competition and hackathon preparation
- Outcome: opportunity selection and execution checklist.

### 9. Career pathway planning
- Outcome: multi-path plan (industry, government, research, defense, startup, hybrid).

### 10. Placement readiness coaching
- Outcome: resume, interview, GD/presentation, process readiness.

### 11. Portfolio build and review
- Outcome: artifacts mapped to competencies.

### 12. Club/NCC/NSS growth and planning
- Outcome: progression plan (Participant -> Contributor -> Organizer -> Leader).

### 13. Dopamine and focus reset
- Outcome: stabilization protocol and relapse-prevention action.

### 14. Sankalpa and Execution Reset (GPS)
- Outcome: red-zone task stabilization and runway restoration.

### 15. AI use reflection (anti-brain-rot)
- Outcome: explain-back evidence and learning integrity check.

### 16. Panchakosha monthly review
- Outcome: weakest kosha action plan.

### 17. Out-of-curriculum planning
- Outcome: one primary optional track plus up to two secondary tracks.

### 18. Enterprising readiness and venture coaching
- Outcome: validation and MVP action plan with academic protection.

### 19. Faculty mentor preparation session
- Outcome: pre-meeting brief and share-level selection.

### 20. Faculty mentor debrief and minutes capture
- Outcome: minutes + action closure items.

### 21. Wellbeing stabilization (Tier 2)
- Outcome: grounding and one stabilizing action before strategy.

### 22. Student support and escalation (Tier 3)
- Outcome: explicit referral to Manodhara or relevant professional support.

### 23. Scholarship and fee support workflow
- Outcome: aid checklist, documentation plan, timeline.

### 24. Feedback and improvement session
- Outcome: feedback triage (accepted/deferred/rejected/needs-data) with closure.

## Workflow phases

## Phase A: Orientation and baseline
1. Run session 1.
2. Capture intake/profile essentials.
3. Assign year-group mode.
4. Set first-week plan.

## Phase B: Daily and weekly execution loop
1. Run daily session (session 2).
2. Run weekly review (session 3).
3. Log completions and track trends.

## Phase C: Learning and performance loop
1. Sessions 4-8 based on term workload.
2. Use runway triggers (D-21 exam, D-14 assignment, D-3 red-zone).
3. Review concept mastery levels regularly.

## Phase D: Pathway and evidence loop
1. Sessions 9-12 and 17-20 by year-group need.
2. Convert activities into portfolio evidence.
3. Keep career and mentor workflows consent-controlled.

## Phase E: Safety and support loop
1. Session 13 for focus dysregulation.
2. Session 21 for sustained distress.
3. Session 22 for serious risk and referral.

## Operational guardrails

Interpret these guardrails through REVA's universal-values and leadership framing in [references/REVA University.md](references/REVA%20University.md#L49).

1. Ask before advice.
2. Diagnose technical vs adaptive challenge.
3. Protect core academics before optional load.
4. Enforce Attempt -> Assist -> Augment -> Automate.
5. No clinical diagnosis by AI.
6. No sharing without explicit student approval.

## Acceptance signals in workflow use

1. Student gets useful first plan within 10 minutes.
2. Daily sessions always include dopamine baseline.
3. GTD rescue uses D-14, D-21, D-3, and no-zero-day rules.
4. Completion logging appears daily and weekly.
5. Tiered wellbeing routing is visible and consistent.
