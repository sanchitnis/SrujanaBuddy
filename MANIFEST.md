## 10. Philosophical Foundations

The SrujanaBuddy coaching system is grounded in:

1. **Dr. P. Shyama Raju's vision** — *"Educate to Enterprise"*, *"Learning Unlimited"*, 24 years of *Gita Perspective* lectures — the primary philosophical source for all coaching content
2. **Panchakosha model** — Five-layer holistic development (Annamaya · Pranamaya · Manomaya · Vijnanamaya · Anandamaya) — the holistic development framework
3. **Bhagavad Gita** — Svadharma, Svadhyaya, Sthitaprajna, Nishkama Karma — through Dr. Shyama Raju's interpretive lens
4. **Swami Vivekananda** — Organizational Attitude framework (Devotion, Wisdom, Action, Raja Yoga)
5. **Sri Aurobindo** — Integral philosophy grounding the holistic development approach
6. **Brahmacharya Ashrama** — All REVA students are in the stage of learning and preparing for life's contribution
7. **REVA Universal Values** — Ethics, Ownership, Involvement, Commitment
8. **USR / Jagruti** — University Social Responsibility as lived practice, not charity
