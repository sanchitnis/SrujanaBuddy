| Month 3–4 | [Theme, e.g. First Build] | [K3]; [K4] | [S3]; [S4] | [C2] | [Course C] |
| Month 5–6 | [Theme, e.g. Portfolio Evidence] | [K5]; [K6] | [S5]; [S6] | [C3] | [Course D] |

**How to fill this section:**
- K (Knowledge): concepts and mental models the student must understand — not just facts.
- S (Skills): concrete things they must be able to do independently by end of the period.
- C (Competencies): holistic demonstrations that integrate K + S — something a mentor or employer can see.
- Linked courses: courses whose content directly feeds one or more KSC items in this period.
