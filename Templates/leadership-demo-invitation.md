## The Challenge We Are Addressing

REVA's mission — "Educate to Enterprise" — requires personalized guidance for each student across learning, competency development, and career pathways. Today, this guidance is limited by faculty availability and time constraints. SrujanaBuddy bridges this gap through:

- **Personalized, always-available coaching** at scale
- **Real-time visibility** into student progress and early warning signals
- **Portfolio-first evidence collection** mapped to competencies
- **Structured support** for Srujana pathway progression (Stages 1–4)

## What SrujanaBuddy Delivers

**For Students:**
- Weekly personalized guidance on learning, time management, and career navigation
- Holistic development support across five dimensions (Panchakosha framework)
- Project-based gamification with visible progress tracking and peer celebration
- Anti-brain-rot guardrails ensuring AI augmentation, not substitution
- Early support before problems become crises (three-tier wellbeing model)

**For Faculty:**
- Frees faculty from repetitive coaching (GTD, dopamine regulation, basic subject tutoring)
- Provides real-time dashboards of student progress and at-risk signals
- Captures structured mentorship minutes and student progress
- Enables faculty focus on advanced mentoring and research collaboration
- Supports all 10+ subject coaching instances per semester

**For REVA as an Institution:**
- Measurable progress on Srujana pathway progression
- Visible portfolio evidence for placements and admissions
- Reduced last-minute academic stress and student burnout
- Strong differentiation in competitive higher education market
- Alignment with institutional mission and holistic development philosophy

## The First Implementation: Grand Challenge Studio (GCS)

We are launching this system with **GCS**, a 2-credit elective for 2nd-semester B.Tech students featuring:

- **6-dimensional Enterprising Ability analytics** tracking student growth in investigation, pivoting, collaboration, prototyping, reflection, and consistency
- **Weekly coaching loop** with Socratic tutoring aligned to course outcomes
- **Public leaderboards** (opt-in) celebrating diverse achievement types
- **Integration** with 4 linked courses for end-to-end competency development
- **Srujana Stage 3 gateway** — all GCS completers reach Creation stage; exceptional students flagged for enterprise/research tracks
