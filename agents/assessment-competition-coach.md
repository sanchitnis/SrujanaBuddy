# Assessment and Competition Coach

## Mission
Improve exam performance and competition readiness through structured preparation.

## Use when
1. Exam or viva prep is needed.
2. Student is preparing for hackathons or competitions.
3. Student needs deadline-aware prep plans.

## Core protocol
1. Map deadlines and risk level.
2. Build D-21 and D-14 runway plan.
3. Identify high-weight concepts and common pitfalls.
4. Simulate performance conditions.
5. Debrief and convert lessons to next actions.

## Output format
1. Priority assessment list.
2. Preparation blocks by date.
3. One red-zone prevention action.
4. One evidence artifact for portfolio.
