### ---

**3\. Course Objectives (COb)**

* **Problem Identification:** Enable students to identify real-world societal, technical, or entrepreneurial problems.

* **Structured Approaches:** Introduce design thinking, systems thinking, and agile-style teamwork.

* **AI Integration:** Familiarize students with the effective and responsible use of AI tools (Copilots/Agents) for ideation and prototyping.

* **Professional Skills:** Develop leadership, communication, and peer collaboration in a studio environment.

* **Reflective Learning:** Help students assess progress and challenges through continuous evaluation.

**6\. Evaluation Scheme**

Evaluation follows a **Growth & Grit** philosophy, rewarding resilience and the ability to learn from failure.

#### **Continuous Assessment (Internal)**

* **Review 1 (Week 3):** 10 Marks (Focus: CO1, CO2, CO5).

* **Review 2 (Week 5):** 10 Marks (Focus: CO2, CO5).

* **Review 3 (Week 9):** 10 Marks (Focus: CO3, CO5, CO6).

* **Review 4 (Week 12):** 20 Marks (Focus: CO4, CO5, CO6).

#### **Summative Assessment**

* **SEE \- Exhibition (Week 14):** 50 Marks (Focus: CO1-CO6).

#### **The "Growth & Grit" Rubric**

| Category | Weight | Success Criteria |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Evidence of Pivot** | 30% | Identifying moments of failure and the logic used to change direction. |
| **Investigation Depth** | 25% | Evidence of interviews and data gathering beyond simple web searches. |
| **Collaboration** | 20% | Incorporating peer feedback and helping others solve roadblocks. |
| **Final Prototype** | 15% | Functional representation of research (perfection not required). |
| **Reflection** | 10% | Articulating personal growth as a problem-solver. |
