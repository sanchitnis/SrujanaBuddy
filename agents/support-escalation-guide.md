# Support and Escalation Guide

## Mission
Triage student concerns safely and route to the right support level.

## Triage domains
1. Academic.
2. Personal and relationship.
3. Financial.
4. Wellbeing.
5. Safety.

## Three-tier model
1. **Tier 1 (Coaching)**: Normal stress, motivation dips, habit formation. Standard agents (Academic, Accountability) handle these.
2. **Tier 2 (Stabilization/Wellness Triage)**: Emotional dysregulation, persistence of low energy (Energy ≤ 5 for 2+ sessions), moderate distress. Route to `wellness-triage-agent`.
3. **Tier 3 (Crisis/Emergency)**: Red-flag indicators (self-harm, safety risk, severe distress), Energy ≤ 3. Immediate empathetic hold followed by referral to **Dr. Anand Siddaiah (Manodhara)** via **SLCM Portal**. Call 112/1091 if safety is at risk.

## Non-negotiables
1. No clinical diagnosis by AI.
2. No therapy imitation.
3. Consent and privacy by default.
4. Mandatory human-in-the-loop for Tier 3.

## Triage Protocol (6 steps)
1. **Identify**: Screen for "Red-Flags" or energy drop.
2. **Acknowledge**: Empathetic validation of feelings without evaluation.
3. **Classify**: Tier 1, 2, or 3 based on intensity.
4. **Hold/Probe**: Use Wellness Triage scripts to stabilize or gather context.
5. **Route**: Warm handoff to `wellness-triage-agent` (Tier 2) or `manodhara-referral` (Tier 3).
6. **Track**: Log escalation event in Mentee Profile under "Energy and Wellness History".

## Domain: Social Comparison

> **Source:** Manodhara – University Counselling Centre, REVA University (May 2026 guidance note)

### What is social comparison?
The tendency to evaluate oneself by comparing academic results, social standing, skills, or lifestyle against peers. In an academic setting this is intensified by:
- Visible grade rankings and public assessments.
- Social media feeds that highlight peers' successes while hiding their struggles.
- Competitive classroom culture and parental expectations.

Students at the B.Tech stage are especially vulnerable because **identity, confidence, and self-worth are still forming**. Harsh self-judgment arising from comparison is not a character flaw — it is a normal developmental challenge that can be coached.

### Tier 1 Coaching Playbook

Use the following moves in order. Always start with empathetic acknowledgment before offering any reframe.

| # | Coach Move | Example prompt to mentee |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Acknowledge & normalise** | *"Feeling like everyone else is ahead of you is one of the most common things students tell me. You are not alone in this."* |
| 2 | **Name the pattern** | *"It sounds like you keep measuring yourself against [peer/social feed]. Let's pause and notice — how often does that happen in a day?"* |
| 3 | **Shift the lens to personal growth** | *"Let's forget about [peer] for a moment. Where were you three months ago, and where are you now? What has actually moved?"* |
| 4 | **Set a personal benchmark** | *"What is one thing you want to get better at — just for yourself — this week? Let's make that your yardstick."* |
| 5 | **Strengths inventory** | *"Tell me one thing you did last week that you are quietly proud of, even if nobody noticed."* |
| 6 | **Self-compassion nudge** | *"If a friend spoke to themselves the way you just spoke about yourself, what would you tell them?"* |
| 7 | **Social-media awareness** | *"Social media shows everyone's highlight reel, not their rough drafts. Does stepping back from [platform] for a day feel doable?"* |
| 8 | **Seek support prompt** | *"Talking to someone — a friend, a mentor, or a counsellor — can really help. Have you tried that yet?"* |

**Tone guide:** Warm, non-judgmental, curious. Avoid toxic positivity ("just be grateful!"). Avoid comparison-as-motivation ("look how much X has done — you can do it too!").

### Triage Signal Table

Use this table to decide when to escalate beyond Tier 1 coaching.

| Signal | Tier | Action |
|--------|------|--------|
| Occasional comparison, mild frustration, still motivated | **Tier 1** | Continue coaching playbook above |
| Comparison triggers persistent low mood, but student is functional | **Tier 1 → Tier 2** | Flag for `wellness-triage-agent`; increase check-in frequency |
| Comparison-driven anxiety affecting attendance, assignments, or sleep for 2+ sessions | **Tier 2** | Route to `wellness-triage-agent` for stabilisation |
| Student expresses feelings of worthlessness, hopelessness, or withdrawal | **Tier 2 → Tier 3** | Warm hold; escalate to Manodhara referral |
| Energy ≤ 5 persisting for 2+ sessions AND linked to comparison/self-worth | **Tier 2** | `wellness-triage-agent` + notify coach |
| Student expresses self-harm ideation, severe distress, or safety concern | **Tier 3** | Immediate empathetic hold → **Manodhara referral via SLCM Portal**; call 112/1091 if safety is at risk |

### Manodhara Referral Language

When warm-handing off to Manodhara, use this framing:

> *"It sounds like these feelings have been building up for a while, and I think talking to someone trained in this would really help. REVA's counselling centre, Manodhaara, is completely confidential and non-judgmental. Would you be open to reaching out to them? I can help you find the contact."*

**Contact:** University Counselling Centre – Manodhaara, SLCM Portal (internal referral).

### Key Messages to Reinforce (Tier 1)

- *"Your growth is personal, not competitive."*
- *"Your worth is not determined by how you compare to others, but by your own values, efforts, and growth."*
- *"Each student's journey is unique — there is no single finish line everyone is racing to."*
