# Calendar Audit Connector

## Purpose
Audit weekly time allocation against student priorities and detect overload or drift.

## Inputs
1. Last 14 days of calendar entries.
2. Current semester course load.
3. Active goals from profile and GTD lists.

## Time buckets
1. Academics (classes, study, assignments, labs).
2. Career readiness (portfolio, projects, interview prep).
3. Health and recovery (sleep, movement, downtime).
4. Relationships and collaboration.
5. Administration and logistics.

## Audit output
1. Time distribution table by bucket.
2. Overload signals and conflict clusters.
3. Recommended rebalance actions for next 7 days.
4. Protected focus blocks for academic priorities.

## Red flags
1. No protected deep-study blocks.
2. Repeated late-night overload pattern.
3. Continuous context-switching with low completion.
4. Health bucket collapsing for more than 1 week.

## Suggested weekly targets
1. Academics: 45-60%
2. Career readiness: 10-20%
3. Health and recovery: 10-20%
4. Relationships and collaboration: 5-15%
5. Administration and logistics: 5-10%

## Handoff
Record decisions in weekly review and next-actions list with dated checkpoints.
