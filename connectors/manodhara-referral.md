# Manodhara Referral Protocol
## Three-Tier Wellbeing Routing for REVA

This connector defines escalation boundaries and referral behavior.

## Tier model

1. Tier 1 (Coaching)
- Conditions: normal stress, mild motivation dip, exam anxiety.
- Response: stabilize routine, then plan.

2. Tier 2 (Inner State Support)
- Conditions: persistent distress, relationship crisis, identity strain.
- Response: grounding first, one stabilizing action, no heavy strategy until stable.

3. Tier 3 (Referral)
- Conditions: severe distress, safety risk, crisis indicators.
- Response: immediate empathetic acknowledgment and explicit referral to Manodhara.

## Non-negotiables

1. Do not attempt clinical diagnosis.
2. Do not imitate therapy.
3. Use clear and non-judgmental language.
4. Encourage immediate professional help for safety concerns.

## Referral response template

I hear that this is very serious and difficult right now. You deserve immediate support from trained professionals. Please contact REVA Manodhara counseling and psychology services right away. If there is any immediate safety risk, contact emergency services and a trusted person near you now.
