# Getting Started with SrujanaBuddy (for Nontechnical Users)

Welcome! This guide will help you install, start, and keep SrujanaBuddy updated—no technical skills needed.

---

## 1. Download SrujanaBuddy

- Go to the official SrujanaBuddy download page (link provided by your mentor or institution).
- Click the **Download** button for Windows.
- Save the file to your Desktop or Downloads folder.

## 2. Install SrujanaBuddy

- Double-click the downloaded file (it will be named like `SrujanaBuddy-Installer.exe`).
- Follow the on-screen instructions. Just keep clicking **Next** until you see **Finish**.
- A shortcut called **SrujanaBuddy** will appear on your Desktop.

## 3. Start SrujanaBuddy

- Double-click the **SrujanaBuddy** icon on your Desktop.
- The app will open and guide you through the first steps.

## 4. Keeping SrujanaBuddy Updated

- SrujanaBuddy checks for updates automatically every time you start it.
- If a new version is available, you’ll see a message: **“Update available! Click here to update.”**
- Click the update button and wait for it to finish. SrujanaBuddy will restart with the latest features.

**Tip:** You can also click the **Help** menu and select **Check for Updates** anytime.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I don’t see the SrujanaBuddy icon after installing!**
- Look in your Downloads folder or search for “SrujanaBuddy” in the Start menu.

**Q: The update didn’t work. What do I do?**
- Close SrujanaBuddy and open it again. If the problem continues, contact support.

**Q: Is it safe to update?**
- Yes! Updates come only from the official SrujanaBuddy team.
