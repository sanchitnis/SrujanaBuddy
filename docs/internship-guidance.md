# Internship Guidance for REVA Students

## How to Find the Right Internship

1. **Explore the Internship Portals Database**
   - Use the curated list in [knowledge/internship-portals.json](../knowledge/internship-portals.json) to discover national, international, and company-specific internship portals.
   - Filter by tags/domains (e.g., engineering, management, research) to find relevant opportunities.
   - Visit each portal for the latest openings and eligibility details.

2. **Contact the Career Development Center (CDC) at REVA University**
   - The CDC provides personalized support, exclusive opportunities, and guidance for applications.
   - **Contact Details:**
     - Email: cdc@reva.edu.in
     - Phone: +91-80-1234-5678
     - Office: CDC, REVA University Main Campus
     - Web: https://www.reva.edu.in/cdc
   - Sample outreach template:
     > Subject: Request for Internship Guidance
     >
     > Dear CDC Team,
     >
     > I am a [your year/branch] student interested in pursuing an internship in [domain/industry]. Could you please guide me on available opportunities and the application process?
     >
     > Thank you,
     > [Your Name]

3. **Best Practices**
   - Start early and apply to multiple portals.
   - Prepare a strong resume and cover letter.
   - Follow up with the CDC for exclusive or institution-supported opportunities.
   - Check the portals and CDC updates regularly (at least once per semester).

4. **FAQ**
   - **Q:** How do I know if I am eligible for an internship?
     **A:** Check the eligibility field in the JSON database and on the portal itself.
   - **Q:** Can I get help with my application?
     **A:** Yes, the CDC can review your resume and guide you through the process.
   - **Q:** Are there internships for non-engineering domains?
     **A:** Yes, filter by tags like "business", "arts", "law", etc.
