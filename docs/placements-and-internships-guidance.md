# Placements and Internships Guidance for REVA Students

## What to use

1. **Placements and Internships plugin (optional)**
   - Run `python3 tools/live-data/placements-and-internships/placements_and_internships.py` to filter curated portals by category, tag, and search term.
   - The plugin is optional, uses only Python stdlib, and can run from a local checkout or inside a container with the repository mounted.

2. **Curated portal databases**
   - Internships: [knowledge/internship-portals.json](../knowledge/internship-portals.json)
   - Placements: [knowledge/placement-portals.json](../knowledge/placement-portals.json)

3. **Career Development Center (CDC)**
   - Email: cdc@reva.edu.in
   - Phone: +91-80-1234-5678
   - Office: CDC, REVA University Main Campus
   - Web: https://www.reva.edu.in/cdc

## Suggested workflow

1. Pick a target domain and role family first.
2. Filter the curated portals by tag (`engineering`, `business`, `research`, `startup`, etc.).
3. Apply on at least 3 relevant portals instead of waiting on one source.
4. Send the CDC a short mail asking for campus-specific guidance and resume review.
5. Review applications weekly and update your portfolio evidence after every submission, interview, or project milestone.

## Sample CDC outreach

> Subject: Request for Placement / Internship Guidance
>
> Dear CDC Team,
>
> I am a [your year/branch] student targeting [placement / internship] opportunities in [domain/industry]. Could you please guide me on current openings, eligibility, and the best next step from REVA's side?
>
> Thank you,
> [Your Name]

## Quick reminders

- Start early; don't wait for the final semester itself.
- Tailor resume bullets to the role before every application.
- Keep one evidence tracker for projects, certifications, hackathons, and mock interviews.
- Re-check the portals often because listings move fast.
