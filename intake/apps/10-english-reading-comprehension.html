Passage:
In modern learning environments, students often confuse information access with understanding. Having many resources can create a false sense of progress unless learners actively retrieve ideas, connect them to prior knowledge, and apply them to new problems. Deep learning is less about memorizing isolated facts and more about building structures of meaning. Students who pause to explain concepts in their own words, test themselves, and reflect on mistakes generally retain knowledge longer and transfer it better across subjects.
