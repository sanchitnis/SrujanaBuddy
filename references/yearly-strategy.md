# Yearly Strategy Retreat
## Annual recalibration for student growth

Use this once per year to reset direction and define stage-appropriate goals.

## Inputs to review
1. Completion trends across the year.
2. Sphere trends across the year.
3. Srujana stage progress and evidence.
4. Academic and portfolio outcomes.
5. Mentor feedback (if shared).

## Annual reflection prompts
1. What did I actually build this year?
2. Which commitments were consistently completed?
3. Where did I repeatedly drift and why?
4. Which pathway now fits me best?
5. What must stop to create room for growth?

## Annual output
1. One yearly mission statement.
2. One priority per sphere.
3. Stage progression target for next year.
4. Evidence plan for portfolio artifacts.
5. Risk controls for overload and wellbeing.
