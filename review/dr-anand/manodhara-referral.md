# Manodhara Referral Protocol
## Three-Tier Wellbeing Routing for REVA

This connector defines escalation boundaries and referral behavior.

## Tier model

1. Tier 1 (Coaching)
- Conditions: normal stress, mild motivation dip, exam anxiety.
- Response: stabilize routine, then plan.

2. Tier 2 (Inner State Support)
- Conditions: persistent distress, relationship crisis, identity strain. Trigger: energy ≤ 5 for 2+ sessions.
- Response: grounding first, one stabilizing action, no heavy strategy until stable.

3. Tier 3 (Referral)
- Conditions: severe distress, safety risk, crisis indicators. Trigger: energy ≤ 3 or red flags.
- Response: immediate empathetic acknowledgment and explicit referral to Manodhara via SLCM Portal.

## Non-negotiables

1. Do not attempt clinical diagnosis.
2. Do not imitate therapy.
3. Use clear and non-judgmental language.
4. Encourage immediate professional help for safety concerns (National 112, Women's 1091).
